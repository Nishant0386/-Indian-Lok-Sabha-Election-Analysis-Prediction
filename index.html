Voter Turnout Analysis

Tracking India's democratic participation from the first election to 2024. Turnout rose from 55.5% in 1962 to a peak of 71.3% in 2019, with Lakshadweep consistently leading and Bihar at the bottom.

Delimitation & Women Seats

Women's Reservation Analysis

Key Findings

📈
Turnout

Rose from 55% (1962) to 66%+. Lakshadweep highest; Bihar lowest.

🏛️
Party Shift

BJP went 2 seats (1984) → 303 (2019). INC collapsed 414 → 52.

👤
Candidate Profile

Criminal candidates win MORE. Wealthy candidates win MORE. Incumbents win MORE.

🎯
Margins

~30% seats are swing (<5% margin). These decide who forms government.

🤖
ML Model

Best model predicts at 92.91% accuracy. Total Assets is #1 predictor of winning.

🗺️
Delimitation

UP gains +15 seats; Tamil Nadu loses -7. UP gets +5 women seats, Tamil Nadu LOSES -2.

♀️
Women

Women win at higher rate than men. But only 14% MPs today. 33% reservation = 181 women MPs.
